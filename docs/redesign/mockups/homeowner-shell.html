Homeowner app — direction

A consumer app, not an admin tool. Mental model: book → watch → pay. It should feel warm, calm, and reassuring (the opposite of the dense operator console). The homeowner only creates one thing — a cleaning request — so that's the single prominent action; everything else is glanceable status. Does this consumer vibe feel right?

What defines the homeowner vibe

  • Reassurance hero: "Your next cleaning" is the centerpiece — date, service, cleaner's face, status pill. They open the app to know it's handled.
  • One creation action: "Request a cleaning" is the only thing they make — given a clear button and a persistent FAB.
  • Status, not management: pending requests and history are glanceable; no dense tables, no triage.
  • Warm + calm: softer canvas, friendly greeting, rounded hero with the brand gradient. Consumer-grade, not enterprise.
  • Bottom nav: Home, Cleanings, Messages, Account. Properties, payment methods, history, and browse-services live under Account.
